## Completed source and documentation work

Three bounded workflow fixes are committed:

- Cold offline message Retry prepares storage before outbound admission, checks
  cancellation between those steps, and submits the retry once. It does not
  make an offline retry succeed without a usable transport.
- iOS restoration preparation runs on the existing native queue barrier, not
  the main actor. Native launch still schedules restoration before JavaScript;
  authorization, coalescing and protected-data classification remain unchanged.
  Generation validation runs before and after preparation. This cannot
  interrupt a synchronous Rust preparation already
  inside the queue barrier and does not promise immediate Stop in that case.
- Connection checks belong to their screen and observer lifetime. Blur,
  unmount, target/generation changes, observer release and explicit Stop cancel
  the read. Late results do not update the departed screen. Admitted writes and
  retained announcements deliberately keep their separate durable ownership.

The workspace guide now owns setup; platform guides, validation and the
[roadmap](../docs/roadmap.md) have separate owners. Prior validation and unique
scratch plans were archived without erasing failures or requalifying devices.

622,592-byte firmware region. No memory layout, feature capacity or gate changed.
