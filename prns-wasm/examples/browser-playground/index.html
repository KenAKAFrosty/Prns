This deliberately small playground runs the shared Rust engine in WebAssembly and lets you opt into Auto Wi-Fi, WebSocket, Bluetooth, or Auto USB when you are ready.

A live demo, not a messaging client. It registers an LXMF delivery destination, sends announces, and exposes enough live state to see the interfaces work.
